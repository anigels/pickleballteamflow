Support

Answers to common questions and troubleshooting tips.

Getting Started

Enter between 8 and 24 player names, select the number of courts and rounds, and choose Generate Court Assignments.

Scanning Player Names

Pickleball Team Flow can use a photo to recognize player names. For best results, use a clear, well-lit image with easily readable text.

Camera Problems

If camera access was previously denied, open your device Settings, find Pickleball Team Flow, and allow camera access.

Incorrect Name Recognition

Name recognition may not always identify every name correctly. You can edit the player roster after names have been extracted from an image.

Privacy

For information about how Pickleball Team Flow handles player names and photos, please review our Privacy Policy.

If you have feedback or would like to report a problem, we'd love to hear from you.
