Features

Everything Your Group Needs

Stop arguing over who sits out. Pickleball Team Flow handles all the math so you can focus on playing.

Smart Player Rotation

Rotates partners and opponents each round so everyone gets varied matchups and sit-outs are shared fairly.

Multi-Court Support

Configure as many courts as you have available. Assignments are generated for all courts at once.

Scan Names from Photo

Point your camera at a sign-up sheet and the app will extract player names automatically.

Live Substitutions

Swap a player mid-round with a single tap. Late arrivals and early departures are no problem.

How It Works
